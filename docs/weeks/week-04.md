# Week 4 · The CPU: decode and encode

Week 4's class moved into week 5. Its two build steps are there:

- **`decode`** is step 1 of Week 5: 4 bytes in, an instruction (or an error) out.
- **`encode`** is step 2 of Week 5: `decode` backwards, which the assembler uses.

Open Week 5's **Build** tab: both steps are in its list, with their prompts and tests.
